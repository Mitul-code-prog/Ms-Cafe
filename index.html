Menu

1. M’s Signature Flat White

Velvety double shot espresso with microfoam milk — strong but smooth

3. Avocado Bauernbrot

Toasted German farmhouse bread topped with smashed avocado, chili flakes, and lemon.

Viennese-style coffee with steamed milk, served with a warm mini apple strudel.

Cinnamon roll flavored latte with vanilla, brown sugar, and a dusting of real cinnamon.
